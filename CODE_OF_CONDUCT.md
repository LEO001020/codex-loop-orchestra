# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level,
or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Respect privacy and never publish credentials, private paths, or session data.
- Accept maintainer decisions on safety, release gates, and repository scope.
- Help make technical discussions welcoming to new contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disclosure
of private information, sustained disruption, and knowingly unsafe publication
of credentials or vulnerabilities are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Report
conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method on the
GitHub profile of `LEO001020`. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and
confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
